7. Recommended Regimens for Treatment of Adults and Children with Drug-Susceptible TB (Pulmonary TB)

View in chapter → Special Clinical Situations

Last Updated October 2025

Note:
  • Duration of therapy for patients with drug-susceptible TB should be extended to 9 months (31 weeks continuation phase, 39 weeks total) for patients who have cavitary pulmonary TB and remain sputum culture positive after 2 months of therapy.
  • Many experts would extend therapy to 9 months for all patients with HIV/TB, especially those slow to convert to negative cultures or not on effective HIV treatment.
